Dark America 2025

After escaping the madness that consumed California in 2019, she finally returns home. Six years later, a sudden wave of attacks erupts across the state and her name is connected to every one of them.

Framed for the crimes, nowhere to go, nowhere to hide. She becomes the most wanted person in the state. With rogue cops and a depraved gang from the west hunting her down, the madness she thought was left behind has followed her home.

But this time, she's not completely alone.

Major Matthew - who helped provide protection after the escape.

Michael Sorrow - an informer who provided a cover for years. The only contact the she can rely on while uncovering the truth and clearing her name.

There is only one night to fix everything. Once the sun comes up, it’s over.

Ascend into the clarity as you fight through the dark streets and alleys of the east. A dark atmospheric FPS action story on enhanced vanilla GoldSrc engine.



Sequel to unreleased horror game 'Tenebrific - The Dark Hope'
